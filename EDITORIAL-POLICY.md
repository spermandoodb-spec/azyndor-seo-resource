# Editorial Policy

- Articles must serve a defined reader need.
- Claims must be verified or clearly qualified.
- Never invent performance tests, ratings, user feedback, or credentials.
- Links must be contextual, accurate, and useful to readers.
- Disclose material relationships.
- Do not publish scraped, spun, or mass-produced doorway pages.
- Correct errors and update time-sensitive guidance.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and applicable law.
