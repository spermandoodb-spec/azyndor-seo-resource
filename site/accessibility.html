Accessibility Fundamentals for Video Experiences

Accessible video design helps people use a service across devices, input methods, and access needs.

Captions

Provide synchronized, accurate captions for dialogue and meaningful sound information. Check timing and speaker identification.

Keyboard controls

Player controls should be reachable and usable without a mouse. A visible focus indicator should show the current position.

Visual presentation

Use sufficient contrast, readable text, and controls that remain identifiable at different screen sizes. Do not rely on color alone.

Responsive design

Test mobile widths and orientation changes. Controls should have adequate touch targets and should not overlap important content.
